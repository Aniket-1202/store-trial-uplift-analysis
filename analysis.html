How to read this analysis

The workflow, from pre-period matching to a cautious estimate
1Define the windows

Weeks 1–48 are pre-period; weeks 49–52 are the pilot window.

2Match before the trial

Each pilot store is paired to one unique control using pre-trial sales and customer counts.

3Compare the changes

Subtract each control’s pre-to-trial change from its pilot’s change.

4Show uncertainty

Resample matched pairs 2,000 times with a fixed seed; summarize the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles.

Interpret with care

These numbers come from generated data, not a real retailer. The 7.31% estimate is illustrative and is not a verified business outcome or a causal conclusion. Greedy matching may leave imbalance; only eight stores were piloted; pre-trends, spillovers, concurrent changes, statistical power, and all time-series uncertainty are not assessed. The interval describes resampling across matched pairs only. A real experiment needs a suitable design and qualified review.
